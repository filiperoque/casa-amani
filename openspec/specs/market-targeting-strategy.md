# Casa Amani — Direct-flight + overnight-stay targeting strategy

Source of truth for content localisation, ad targeting, and seasonal content planning.
See the full spec in conversation history. Key decisions below.

## Tier 1 markets (own completely)
1. UK — #1 foreign market, multiple year-round routes, default EN content
2. Germany — #2, DE-language landing page justified
3. France — #3, FR landing page justified
4. Poland — #4 (upgraded after DREM data), PL landing page is first-mover
5. Mainland Portugal — 14.2% of stays, PT-PT essential

## Language priority
1. English (non-negotiable)
2. Portuguese PT-PT (non-negotiable)
3. German (biggest ROI second language)
4. French (#3 foreign market)
5. Polish (first-mover advantage)
6. Dutch (nice-to-have, EN covers them)

## Seasonal content windows
- Jan–Mar: winter sun, target UK/DE/Nordics, remote-work angle
- Apr–Jun: shoulder, strongest organic content window
- Jul–Sep: high season + US Newark route, family travel
- Oct–Dec: shoulder + Christmas escape

- Cruise passengers (728k in 2024, not addressable)
